# FACKTS Music — Legal implementation sign-off checklist

Prepared 25 October 2026. These pages are preview-only drafts, not legal certification or evidence of operational compliance.

## Site surfaces changed
- Public footer: direct Privacy Notice, Terms of Use and Copyright & Takedown links.
- /privacy, /terms, /copyright: substantive notices with contact and enforcement routes.
- Registration checkbox: links directly to policy texts while retaining required server-side acceptance.
- Contact and partnership forms: disclose the existing CRM workflow and link to privacy notice.
- Sitemap: legal pages discoverable.

## Owner and counsel decisions before publishing
1. Verify full legal name, postal address and controller/processor responsibilities for FACKTS Music / FACKTS Africa; approve ODPC registration status, category and any DPO details. Do not invent an ODPC registration number.
2. Map personal data categories and actual vendors: Supabase authentication/database, Vercel hosting, object storage, CRM integrations, email and analytics; establish transfer locations, legal bases and contracts.
3. Approve specific retention and erasure schedule, secure data subject access/export/correction/deletion handling and accountable response process.
4. Audit nonessential cookies and analytics. Add opt-in consent and control only if required by actual implementations; session cookies needed for authentication are a different category.
5. Confirm age eligibility, procedures for parental/guardian permission, image releases and children's data before inviting minors.
6. Review contributor agreements, permission for samples, covers, photographs, videos, performances, masters and compositions. Define separate rights for private collaborator access versus public listening/distribution.
7. Create an operational notice and response owner for copyright allegations, counter-notices, preserving audit records and escalation, including KECOBO/court processes where appropriate.
8. Review public claims: commercial rights transfers, payouts, licensed music distribution and guaranteed security must not be asserted unless operationally supported.
9. Add affirmative consent collection only for activities for which consent is the proper legal basis; do not bundle optional marketing consent into mandatory service terms.
10. Review the published language with qualified Kenyan counsel and set responsible officer and periodic review schedule.
